Who We Are

Igniting the Entrepreneurial Spirit

E-Cell is the official entrepreneurship body of our college, dedicated to creating an ecosystem where innovation thrives and future leaders are born.

Our Mission

To cultivate a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students by providing mentorship, resources, and a platform to turn ideas into reality.

Our Vision

To be the foremost hub of student innovation, recognized nationally for producing impactful startups that solve real-world problems and create lasting change.

Our Community

A vibrant network of 200+ students, mentors, and alumni united by a common passion for building businesses that matter and making a difference.

What We Do

We host hackathons, workshops, speaker sessions, pitch competitions, and startup boot camps to sharpen skills and connect students with investors.
