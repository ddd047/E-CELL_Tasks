Who We Are

Igniting the Entrepreneurial Spirit

E-Cell UIET Kurukshetra is the official entrepreneurship body of our college, dedicated to creating an ecosystem where innovation thrives and future leaders are born.

Our Mission

To cultivate a culture of innovation by providing mentorship, resources, and a platform to turn ideas into reality.

Our Vision

To be the foremost hub of student innovation at UIET, recognized for producing startups that solve real-world problems.

Our Community

A vibrant network of 200+ students, mentors, and alumni united by a shared passion for building businesses that matter.

What We Do

We host hackathons, workshops, speaker sessions, pitch competitions, and startup boot camps to sharpen skills.
